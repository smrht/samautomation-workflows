# Privacy

Samautomation Workflows is a skills package, maintained by Samautomation. It has no server connection, account system, analytics or telemetry. The included Python helper reads the local files you give it, writes its report to standard output and makes no network requests. We do not receive those files through this package.

Your chosen AI host and any separately connected tools process information under their own policies. Review files before sharing them with an AI host or in a public support issue. Do not upload credentials or private customer data. Executing a workflow or browsing a website can contact external services when you separately authorize that action; those actions are not performed by the bundled checker.

Support: https://github.com/smrht/samautomation-workflows/issues. Use a minimal anonymized reproduction.
